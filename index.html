Our story

Suraj & Deeksha

He first saw her in a crowded college corridor, laughing with her friends, and forgot where he was going. A borrowed notebook turned into a two-hour chat, and the chats turned into late-night calls and shared chai.

Exams, distance, and family questions tested them, but every bad day ended with the same person on the phone.

Years later, he took her to the place where they first really talked. He had planned a long speech, but forgot all of it and only asked, "Will you spend your life with me?" She said yes before he finished.

Now, two families are coming together to celebrate their story.
